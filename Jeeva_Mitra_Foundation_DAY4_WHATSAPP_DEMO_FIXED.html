OUR MISSION

What We Actually Do

Jeeva Mitra Foundation works to turn compassion into practical support for people and communities who need it.

🏠

Orphanage Support

We provide responsible support, useful resources and assistance to orphanages, helping create a caring and supportive environment for children.

🤝

Old Age Home Support

We support old-age homes through essential resources, care initiatives and community involvement that promote dignity and companionship.

🌱

Social Wellness

We take part in social-wellness initiatives that encourage kindness, community support and a better quality of life.
